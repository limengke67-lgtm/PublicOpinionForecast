# Attribution and modifications

PublicOpinionForecast is a derivative of [MiroFish](https://github.com/666ghj/MiroFish), originally published by 666ghj and upstream contributors under AGPL-3.0.

The local research baseline is commit `7657031ac01184afe2cb220f5ee3545573b5e843`.

Modifications introduced for this graduation project include:

- Immutable forecast verification and time-aligned observation intake.
- LLM usage accounting, request limits, preparation reuse and concurrency controls.
- Causal rolling numeric calibration, same-information baselines and historical replay.
- Event-centred workflow management, consolidated evidence reports and delivery checks.

The original license remains in `LICENSE`. Original README documents are retained in `docs/upstream/`. Upstream names appearing in the original simulator and assets identify their source; they do not imply that the upstream authors endorse this derivative or its research claims.

PHEME data attribution and sampling limitations are documented separately in `docs/datasets/pheme/README.md`.
